SUMIF & COUNTIF

Conditional analysis

FormulaSyntax
SUMIF=SUMIF(A:A,"North",C:C)
SUMIFS=SUMIFS(C:C,A:A,"North",B:B,">1000")
COUNTIF=COUNTIF(A:A,"Paid")
COUNTIFS=COUNTIFS(A:A,"Delhi",B:B,">1000")
